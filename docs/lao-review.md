# Lao text review sheet

For review by the Lao Association of the Deaf (or other native Lao readers) before field use.
Every piece of Lao text the app shows is listed here with its English meaning.

For each row, mark **OK** or write a better wording in **Suggested Lao**. Please also say if a phrase is too long to read at a glance: alerts must be understood in about one second.

The app team applies changes in `js/labels.js` (alert labels) and `index.html` / `js/app.js` (interface text).

## 3. Questions for reviewers

1. Are the three level words (ອັນຕະລາຍ / ລະວັງ / ຂໍ້ມູນ) the words deaf readers would use?
2. Do the icons match what a Lao reader expects (for example 📯 for a vehicle horn, 🥁 for a temple drum)?
3. Can the three vibration patterns be told apart without looking? (Try them in Settings → test buttons, or on guide page 2.)
4. Which everyday Lao sounds are missing? Those become recordings for the starter pack (`tools/build-pack.mjs`).
